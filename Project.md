# Excel Expense Tracker

A personal expense and budget tracker built in Excel to analyse spending by category, payment mode and month.

- dashboard <a href="https://github.com/Shadab-khan-s118/Excel-Project_Hub/blob/main/Screenshot%202026-10-28%20171159.png">dashboard View</a>

## What it includes
- *Data sheet:* 48 expenses (Jan to Oct 2026) with dropdowns for Category and Payment Mode (data validation)
- *Summary sheet:* total, average, highest, lowest and count, plus per-category totals
- *Budget sheet:* budget vs actual with an Over Budget / Within Budget status
- *Pivot sheet:* spend by category and payment mode, with a month filter
- *Dashboard:* key numbers, a pivot chart and a pie chart
